# Fixture revision and scope

The first 45-degree experiment used a 100 mm cube. It failed the coefficient tolerance in three of thirty cases (x_tile, x_carpet, x_tarmac). Its aspect ratio left limited resistance to tipping on the incline; it was not accepted as an engine calibration fixture.

The accepted fixture uses a 400 x 400 x 20 mm, 1 kg slider, with matching rectangular collision geometry and analytical inertia. Acceleration is fitted from world poses over 0.5-2.5 simulated seconds. Its updated 30-case experiment passed the declared absolute-mu tolerance 0.05 and position fit RMSE 0.01 m. Raw messages, exact SDF and the report are retained here.

This is not a slip-versus-force tyre sweep, a drawbar test, deformable ground validation or hardware calibration. Measurements apply to this engine/configuration/fixture. Static/kinetic transition remains simplified by native Coulomb contact.
