The three batches together contain34 flat-print bed layouts:12+14+8, not three physical prints. The full-scale assembly is much larger than the bed; parts have been split or rotated to preserve full scale and allow predominantly flat printing. This layout is not claimed to use the fewest possible beds. Keeping thin moving components mostly flat avoids making the important links depend on strength between upright layers.

Files contain meshes, not nozzle temperatures or printer toolpaths. Use a calibrated PLA profile for a room-temperature unloaded hand test, a0.4mm nozzle,0.20mm layers, five walls and five top/bottom layers. See the batch settings below. Brims/supports are generated in the slicer and are not modeled into these STLs. Use a5mm brim for tall narrow supports. TEST BEFORE BATCH PRINTING
These are prototype adaptations, not a physically tested kit. New lap/strip joints and hardware protrusions must clear neighboring links over the complete travel. Test one corner and one plate joint before printing the whole kit. Start unloaded and move the mechanism slowly by hand. Stop if the mechanism binds, joints slip, or plastic flexes/cracks. No physical fit test, dynamic interference simulation, FEA, load rating or slicer run was performed.

WHAT IS EXCLUDED
Leg/suspension/wheel modules are excluded. Your already-printed telescope stages, named guideL/guideR parts and rot/hexrot rotary links are excluded. Screws, nuts, pins, bearings, motors, electronics and the camera itself are real hardware, not plastic STL parts. The four modeled hex follower sleeves have OD6.2mm, ID6.0mm and length4mm, giving only0.1mm wall; a printable substitute is not supplied. Obtain fitted sleeves or redesign that interface after measuring the actual pins/slots.

VERIFICATION
Every bed STL has watertight, consistently wound meshes and the expected count of separate connected components. All bounds are inside X/Y8..242mm and Z0..241mm. Part bounding boxes do not overlap. New joint holes were checked to remain within the original material. The eight integrated hex halves were checked to reconstruct the original fused source geometry after reassembly, apart from their new fastener holes. These checks establish geometry/mesh integrity, not motion or strength.
